Question 1 — Mr Ivan Ho (15 marks)

Scenario15 marks

Ivan Ho is employed on an annual salary of $1,020,000 for the year of assessment 2026/27. During the year he incurred $60,000 on a job-related course, qualifying as a deductible self-education expense, and made the mandatory MPF contribution of $18,000. He is married; his wife has no income of her own.

Ivan also wholly owns a separate residential flat, which he lets out unconnected with his employment. Its net assessable value for the year (after rates and the 20% statutory allowance) is $200,000. He pays mortgage interest of $150,000 a year on the loan used to acquire that flat.

(a) Compute Ivan's salaries tax liability for the year, ignoring any election for personal assessment. (10 marks)

(b) Advise, with supporting calculations, whether Ivan should elect personal assessment for the year. (5 marks)

Question 2 — XYZ Trading Ltd (25 marks)

Scenario25 marks

XYZ Trading Ltd, a Hong Kong company with no connected entities, reports a net profit per accounts of $8,000,000 for the year ended 31 March 2027. The accounts include depreciation of $450,000, approved charitable donations of $200,000, and legal fees of $60,000 for acquiring a new five-year retail lease.

The company's plant and machinery pool (20% pool) had a written-down value brought forward of $600,000; during the year it bought qualifying plant for $800,000 and sold plant for $100,000. Separately, it incurred $1,500,000 of qualifying Type B R&D expenditure, already charged as an expense (and so already included) in the $8,000,000 net profit above. The company also has an unrelieved trading loss of $500,000 brought forward from an earlier year.

(a) Compute XYZ Trading Ltd's profits tax payable for the year. (20 marks)

(b) XYZ Trading Ltd is considering whether the $500,000 loss could instead be surrendered to a profitable fellow group company. Advise, with reasons. (5 marks)

Total for part (a): 20 marks. Stating the total enhanced R&D deduction ($4,500,000) instead of the additional amount still to be deducted ($3,000,000), and computing the annual allowance on the pool before deducting the initial allowance, are the two errors this question is designed to catch.

(b) (5 marks): Hong Kong has no group loss relief of any kind. Each company, including XYZ Trading Ltd and any fellow group company, is a separate person chargeable to profits tax in its own right (2 marks). The $500,000 loss cannot be surrendered to, or set off against, a fellow group company's profits, however profitable that company is or however closely the two are connected (2 marks). The loss simply remains within XYZ Trading Ltd itself, carried forward indefinitely with no time limit, to be set off only against XYZ's own future assessable profits from the same trade (1 mark).
